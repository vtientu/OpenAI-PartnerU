> **Course:** OpenAI Foundational Knowledge

>
> **Freshness note:** OpenAI's current public Solutions hub (21 September 2026) highlights industry solution areas including **financial services, healthcare, life sciences, retail, government, and education**. The examples here synthesize recurring patterns across those public materials.

# 3. What changes across industries?

Same model may be used differently because industries differ in:

## Data
EHR vs CRM vs transaction data.

## Language
Clinical terminology vs financial terminology.

## Workflow
Care delivery vs underwriting vs merchandising.

## Systems
EHR vs core banking vs POS.

## Risk
Patient safety vs financial loss vs learning integrity.

## Regulation
Industry-specific obligations.

## KPI
Care quality vs loss ratio vs conversion.

# 33. Case study ≠ template guarantee

A customer story can show:

> someone achieved X with AI.

It does not prove:

> your customer will achieve X.

Use case studies for:

- inspiration;
- hypothesis;
- pattern evidence.

Then validate:

- local workflow;
- local baseline;
- local eval.

---

# 34. Vertical solution statement

A strong vertical solution description:

```text
For [user]
in [industry workflow],
AI will [assist/automate specific task]
using [trusted data/tools],
with [controls/human role],
measured by [KPI].
```

Example:

> For retail store associates, a multilingual knowledge assistant retrieves approved product and policy information to answer shopper questions, with role-based access and source-backed responses, measured by response time and service consistency.

# 36. Common mistakes

1. Copy use case across industries unchanged.
2. Industry page = customer-specific proof.
3. Ignore domain systems.
4. Ignore professional oversight.
5. Use generic KPI.
6. Treat regulation as final-stage check.
7. Over-automate high-impact decisions.
8. Assume same data access everywhere.
9. Recommend product before workflow.
